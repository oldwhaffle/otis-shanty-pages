Artist bio

Otis Shanty is a Boston indie rock band formed by college classmates Sadye Bobbette, Ryan DiLello, Julian Snyder, and Jono Quinn. Their music pairs jangly pop hooks with experimental noise-folk and atmospheric textures. Following the acclaimed 2024 album Up on the Hill, the band returns with Shadow of Doubt, out September 17, 2026.
